GOOD TO KNOW

Little habits, brighter smiles

A pea-sized amount

More paste doesn't clean better — technique does. Half that for kids.

Don't over-rinse

Spit, don't rinse hard — let the fluoride sit and do its work.

Reach the back

Molars do the hardest work and are missed the most. Give them time.

Dry the brush

Rinse well and store upright in the open air, not a closed cup.
