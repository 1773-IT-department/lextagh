OUR COMMITMENT

FLAGSHIP PROGRAMME

Keep Her in School

Every term, Yazz teams visit junior and senior high schools with free sanitary pads, honest conversations about menstrual health, and a message every girl deserves to hear: your period should never cost you your education.
