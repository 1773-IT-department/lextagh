ABOUT LINZZY

Rooted in Care. Inspired by Life. Made for Everyday Moments.

Linzzy is a feminine, maternal and baby care brand under Lexta Ghana Limited, created to support the personal care needs of women, mothers and growing families.

We understand that every stage of life comes with its own experiences, responsibilities and moments that deserve thoughtful attention.

From the confidence a woman seeks during her menstrual cycle to the comfort parents want for their babies, Linzzy is committed to making everyday hygiene and personal care more convenient.

Our product portfolio includes sanitary towels, panty liners, baby diapers and baby wipes, offering practical choices for different care routines.

Our Story, Inspired by the People We Care For

Every woman has a story. Every mother has a journey. Every child begins life with moments worth protecting.

Linzzy draws inspiration from these everyday experiences.

We recognise that personal care is not simply about using a product. It is about feeling comfortable, prepared, understood and supported.

This understanding guides our commitment to developing and offering feminine and baby care essentials that respond to everyday consumer needs.

To provide accessible, dependable and thoughtfully developed feminine hygiene and baby care products while promoting education, responsible care and meaningful community engagement.

Our Commitment to Babies and Families

Our Flagship Initiative: The Safe Bottoms Initiative

Clean Water. Better Hygiene. Brighter Beginnings.

Clean water plays an essential role in maintaining personal hygiene, particularly for families caring for infants and young children.

Yet access to reliable water sources remains a challenge for some communities.

Through The Safe Bottoms Initiative, Linzzy connects its baby care business with efforts to support access to safe water and improved hygiene conditions.

The initiative is designed to contribute funding towards the construction and maintenance of mechanised boreholes in communities experiencing water scarcity.

By connecting everyday consumer purchases with community support, Linzzy seeks to make its commitment to family care meaningful beyond the home.

Our Focus Areas

Access to Clean Water

Supporting community access to reliable water sources through eligible borehole development and maintenance activities.

Infant and Family Hygiene

Encouraging safe hygiene practices that support babies, caregivers and households.

Community Education

Promoting awareness of responsible sanitation, safe water handling and everyday hygiene.

Sustainable Community Support

Encouraging community participation and responsible management of water facilities to support their long-term use.

Supporting Sustainable Development Goal 6

The Safe Bottoms Initiative aligns with SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation, which seeks to improve access to safe water and sanitation for all.

Through this commitment, Linzzy aims to contribute to healthier hygiene environments for families and communities.

Menstrual Health Education and Awareness

Every Period Deserves Understanding, Dignity and Care.

Menstrual health education is an important part of empowering women and girls.

Linzzy seeks to encourage accurate information, positive hygiene habits and open conversations that help reduce menstrual stigma.

Our proposed menstrual health education activities will focus on:

Understanding menstrual hygiene and product usage

Recognising normal menstrual changes and warning signs

Encouraging responsible sanitary product disposal

Supporting confidence through accurate health education

Promoting respectful conversations about menstruation

We believe education can help women and girls make informed decisions about their personal care.

Parenting and Baby Care Education

Supporting the People Who Care for Little Ones.

Every parent deserves access to practical and reliable baby care information.

Through our Tips & Advice platform and Linzzy Parent Corner, we aim to create opportunities for caregivers to learn, share experiences and access expert-reviewed educational content.

Our focus includes diaper-changing routines, baby hygiene, understanding diaper fit, responsible wipes disposal and safe everyday care.
