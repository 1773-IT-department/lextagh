## Notes and conflicts with Lexta's Drive content

- **Unfinished site.** Every product page shows a "coming soon" store notice ("Something big is brewing! Our store is in the works and will be launching soon!"). Prices are 0 GHS. The CSR page shows "No Posts Found!".
- **Theme demo leftovers in the header:** phone "009-215-5599" and "info@example.com" are template placeholders, not Linzzy contacts. The site title is misspelt "Linzzy Poducts". Do not reuse.
- **Diaper copy borrowed from Yazz:** the diaper product says "How to use Yazz baby diaper". The steps are generic and could be reused with the brand name corrected.
- **Safe Bottoms wording differs:** the Linzzy post says "For every diaper sold, Linzzy funds…"; the Lexta site and lextagh.com say "For every jumbo pack sold…". Drive's wording (Yazz and Linzzy diapers and wipes, every jumbo pack) wins.
- **Panty liner pack:** the original says 30 pieces of 155 mm. Check against `../products.md`.
- **Diaper sizes** (Small 44 pcs 3 to 6 kg, Medium 42 pcs 6 to 9 kg, Large 40 pcs 9 to 13 kg, Extra Large 38 pcs over 13 kg) may fill the `[PLACEHOLDER: …]` for Linzzy diaper sizes in `../products.md`, once Lexta confirms them (the original site is not an approved product claim source).
- **Lexta's older Linzzy page** on lextagh.com/linzzy/ repeats the same pad descriptions and still has Latin dummy text ("Sed perspiciatis unde omnis…") under "Heavy Flow, No Leak".
- **Wipes:** the original Linzzy site lists no baby wipes product, although Drive and the Lexta site include Linzzy Baby Wipes. lextagh.com/brands/ calls the wipes "fragrance-free", which Drive contradicts (Rose and Lavender perfume).
- **Emails:** all contact routes on the Linzzy site point to Lexta addresses (Marketing@lextagh.com, Support@lextagh.com). No @linzzyproducts.com address appears anywhere, which bears on the Linzzy inbox "to be confirmed before go-live" (gaps file C1/C7).
