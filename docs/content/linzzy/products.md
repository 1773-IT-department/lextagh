Linzzy Heavy Flow Sanitary Towels

Headline: Extra Care for Your Heavier Days

Some menstrual days require additional protection.

Linzzy Heavy Flow Sanitary Towels are designed for women who prefer higher absorbency during heavier menstrual flow.

Featuring the brand's Absorb, Lock and Breathe design positioning, this variant offers a practical option for managing heavier days while supporting everyday comfort.

Pack Contents:

12 Heavy Flow Sanitary Towels, 290 mm

2 Complimentary Panty Liners, 155 mm

Key Product Features:

Higher-absorbency menstrual protection

290 mm towel length

Designed for heavier-flow days

Includes complimentary panty liners

Comfort-focused design

Recommended Use: Heavier menstrual flow, subject to individual absorbency needs.

How to Use: Remove the individual wrapper, peel away the adhesive backing and position the towel securely inside clean underwear. Change regularly and whenever saturated.

Skin Sensitivity & Allergy Precautions: Discontinue use if itching, burning, redness or irritation develops. Individuals with known sensitivities to adhesives, fragrances or other materials should review the product's ingredient and material information before use.

Linzzy Says: Your heavier days deserve thoughtful care.

Linzzy Baby Diapers

Headline: Made for Little Moves and Everyday Comfort

Every baby has a world to discover.

From stretching little legs to learning how to crawl, babies need diapers that fit comfortably and allow natural movement.

Linzzy Baby Diapers feature a flexible-comfort design intended to support everyday diapering needs.

Product Features:

Flexible Comfort design

Unisex diaper range

Different sizes for growing babies

Disposable diaper format

Designed for everyday baby care

Pack Examples:

Medium (M): 42 pieces, 6 to 9 kg

Large (L): 40 pieces, 9 to 13 kg

Other available sizes and pack quantities should be confirmed against current product packaging before publication.

How to Use:

Place your baby on a clean, safe changing surface.

Remove the soiled diaper and gently clean the diaper area.

Allow the skin to dry.

Position a fresh Linzzy diaper comfortably.

Fasten securely without making it too tight.

Dispose of the used diaper responsibly and wash your hands.

Skin Sensitivity & Allergy Precautions:

A baby's skin can react to moisture, friction or certain product materials.

Check the diaper area regularly and change wet or soiled diapers promptly. Discontinue use if a suspected product-related reaction develops. Consult a healthcare professional for persistent or worsening irritation.

Never leave your baby unattended during a diaper change.

Linzzy Says: Little moves, loving care, brighter beginnings.

Open the resealable lid.

Remove a wipe with clean hands.

Gently clean the intended external skin area.

Avoid contact with eyes, mouth and broken skin. Never flush wipes down the toilet.
