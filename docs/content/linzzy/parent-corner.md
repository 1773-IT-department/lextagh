# Welcome to Linzzy Parent Corner!

### Real Parents. Real Experiences. Shared Wisdom.

Because parenting is a journey worth sharing.

Every little milestone, every unexpected challenge and every precious moment come with a story.

At Linzzy, we believe parenting becomes more meaningful when we learn from one another. That's why we've created Linzzy Parent Corner, a welcoming community space where parents, guardians and caregivers can share their experiences, exchange practical baby care tips, ask questions and celebrate the beautiful journey of raising little ones.

Whether you're navigating your baby's first diaper change, learning new hygiene routines, celebrating a developmental milestone or discovering what works best for your family, your experience could inspire another parent.

Your story matters. Your experience could make someone's parenting journey easier.

## Share Your Parenting Story

Have a parenting experience worth sharing? We'd love to hear it!

From funny diaper-changing moments to helpful baby care discoveries, Linzzy Parent Corner celebrates the everyday experiences that make parenting special.

Submit your story for a chance to be featured in our monthly Parent Corner spotlight on the Linzzy website and selected social media platforms.

### What Can You Share?

My Parenting Journey: Tell us about a memorable experience, challenge or lesson you've learned while caring for your little one.

My Baby Care Tip: Share practical advice on diaper changing, baby wipes, hygiene routines or preparing for outings.

Little Milestones, Big Celebrations: Celebrate a special moment in your child's growth and development.

Ask the Expert: Submit questions about baby hygiene, diapering, skin care or general baby care for consideration in our expert-reviewed Q&A series.

Parenting Myths & Realities: Share common parenting advice you've heard and questions you'd like qualified professionals to clarify.

## How Linzzy Parent Corner Works

Step 1: Share Your Experience

Complete our online submission form with your parenting story, practical tip or baby care question.

Step 2: Our Team Reviews Your Submission

The Linzzy team reviews submissions for relevance, clarity, privacy and suitability for our community.

Step 3: Get Featured

Selected stories and tips may be published in our monthly Parent Corner feature, with your permission.

Step 4: Inspire Other Families

Your experience becomes part of a growing collection of practical parenting knowledge and shared encouragement.

Not every submission will be published, but every contribution helps us understand the topics that matter to parents.

## Website implementation recommendations

The initiative should sit within Tips & Advice Linzzy Parent Corner and include a featured parent story, a monthly question, an expert-reviewed Q&A and a submission form.

For community management, Linzzy should select approximately three to five submissions monthly, subject to participation, consent and editorial review. Expert answers should be reviewed by appropriately qualified professionals before publication.

Suggested submission confirmation message:

Your experience is now part of the conversation at Linzzy Parent Corner.

## Standard Medical Disclaimer for All Articles

Important Baby Care Notice

The information shared in Linzzy Tips & Advice is intended for general educational and awareness purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Every baby's needs and skin sensitivities may differ. Parents and caregivers should consult a qualified healthcare professional for persistent irritation, unusual symptoms or other health concerns. Always follow the instructions and safety guidance provided on Linzzy product packaging.

Recommended publishing schedule: Publish the first four articles over four consecutive weeks, followed by the warm-weather and parenting articles in weeks five and six. Each article can be repurposed into social media carousels, short educational videos and weekly parenting tips.

For website optimisation, use clear search-friendly titles, short article summaries and internal links to the relevant Linzzy Diapers and Linzzy Baby Wipes product pages. Avoid making claims about absorbency, hypoallergenic properties, dermatological testing or other product performance characteristics unless they have been verified.
