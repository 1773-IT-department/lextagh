Six quick Tips & Advice for the website These can appear as short content cards beneath the main articles and be repurposed for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

01 Know Your Flow

Choose sanitary protection according to your bleeding pattern, preferred fit and absorbency needs. Check the product label before use.

Creative headline: Know Your Flow

02 Change Regularly

Change sanitary towels approximately every 4 to 6 hours or sooner when needed. Change panty liners whenever damp or soiled.

Creative headline: Freshness Starts with Good Habits

03 Keep a Period Pouch

Carry spare sanitary towels, clean underwear and a disposal bag to stay prepared at school, work or while travelling.

Creative headline: Prepared Wherever You Go

04 Use Panty Liners Wisely

Panty liners are suitable for light spotting or small amounts of moisture, not for managing heavy menstrual bleeding.

Creative headline: Little Care, Everyday Confidence

05 Listen to Your Skin

Stop using any menstrual product that causes irritation. Fragrance or cooling sensations do not necessarily mean better hygiene.

Creative headline: Comfort Comes First

06 Track Your Cycle

Record your period dates and symptoms to understand your usual pattern and recognise changes worth discussing with a healthcare professional.

Creative headline: Know Your Cycle, Know Your Body
