## Home Care

### Yazz washing powder, 500g / 200g / 5kg / 2kg

Yazz ACTIV DETERGENT WITH ENZYMES: removes stubborn stains.

Caution note: Dissolve Yazz Activ detergent with Enzymes in water and soak clothes for 15 to 20 minutes before washing.

Yazz promise: The newly improved Yazz Activ detergent with Enzymes has been specially formulated to give you BRIGHTER and FRESHER clothes all the time, free from every STAIN. Stay bright, fresher and stain-free with Yazz Activ detergent with Enzymes.

Wash by quantity of clothes against weight, or against the quantity of Yazz Activ. CHECK PACK FOR DIRECTION at all times.

Ingredients: LAS, phosphate, enzymes, optical brightener, sodium sulphate, SCMC, perfume, sodium carbonate.

Precautions:

- Suitable for cotton, linen, polyester and mixtures of those fabrics
- Don't use for silk or wool clothes
- Separate coloured clothes from white and wash them in different basins
- Keep the product in a dry place and out of reach of children
- In case of eye contact, flush with plenty of water
- If swallowed, drink plenty of clean water or milk and seek medical assistance
- In case of irritation on skin, discontinue use and seek medical assistance

For heavy dirt or more clothes, soak for a longer period and use more Yazz Activ detergent accordingly.

CSR: Home Care (Washing Powder/Detergents), SDG 12: Responsible Consumption, "Clean Hands, Green Land".
