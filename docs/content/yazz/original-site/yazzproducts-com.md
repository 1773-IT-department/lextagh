2. My First Period

Your First Period? We've Got Tips Just 4 You.

Your first period is one of the changes that can happen during puberty.

You may feel excited, nervous, confused or have lots of questions. That's okay.

Learning about menstruation before your first period can make the experience feel much more manageable.

What Is a First Period?

Your first menstrual period is called menarche . It is one of the normal changes associated with puberty.

There isn't one exact age when everyone gets their first period, so try not to compare your experience with friends or classmates.

Signs Your Body Is Changing

During puberty, you may notice different physical and emotional changes. These can happen gradually and at different times for different people.

Understanding these changes can help you feel better prepared.

My First Period Checklist

Be ready before your period arrives.

Create a small Yazz® Period Kit for your school bag, handbag or backpack.

Consider including:

- Yazz® Sanitary Pads

- A clean pair of underwear

- Tissues

- A small pouch

- A disposal bag

- Your period tracker or calendar

At School When Your Period Starts?

Don't panic.

Speak with a teacher, school nurse, parent, caregiver or another trusted adult if you need help.

Having a small period kit in your school bag can also help you feel prepared.

Periods shouldn't stop learning.

3. How to Use a Sanitary Pad

Simple Protection. Everyday Confidence.

If you've never used a sanitary pad before, it may seem unfamiliar at first.

A sanitary pad is worn inside your underwear to absorb menstrual flow.

Using Your Yazz® Pad

- Wash and dry your hands.

- Remove the pad from its individual wrapper.

- Peel away the protective backing.

- Position the pad securely in the centre of your underwear.

- If your Yazz® pad has wings, fold them around the sides of your underwear to help keep the pad in place.

- Change your pad regularly and whenever it feels full, uncomfortable or needs replacing.

- Wrap the used pad securely before placing it in an appropriate waste bin.

- Wash your hands afterwards.

Never flush sanitary pads down the toilet.

Always follow the usage and disposal instructions displayed on your Yazz® packaging.

5. Feeling Fresh Every Day

Freshness Begins with Good Personal Hygiene

Taking care of yourself during your period doesn't need to be complicated.

Simple hygiene habits can help you stay comfortable and confident.

Tips Just 4 You

- Change regularly.

Avoid wearing the same sanitary pad for longer than necessary. Change it according to your flow, comfort needs and product directions.

- Wash your hands.

Wash your hands before and after changing your sanitary pad.

- Keep clean.

Regular bathing and appropriate personal hygiene are important throughout your cycle.

- Wear clean underwear.

Fresh, comfortable underwear can help you feel more comfortable throughout your day.

- Carry your essentials.

Keeping an extra pad with you can make unexpected situations easier to manage.

- Dispose responsibly.

Wrap used sanitary pads and place them in an appropriate waste bin. Do not flush them.

Your Period Doesn't Make You Dirty

Menstruation is a normal biological process.

Good menstrual hygiene is about caring for yourself not feeling embarrassed about your period.
