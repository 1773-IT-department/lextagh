### A Little Bag That Can Bring Big Reassurance

A first-period kit is one of the simplest ways to help a young person feel prepared.

It doesn't need to be expensive or complicated.

Choose a small, clean and discreet pouch that fits easily into her school bag.

### The Yazz® First Period Kit

Consider including:

### Sanitary Pads

Pack enough for expected needs, including a spare where practical.

Make sure she knows how to use them before putting them in her kit.

### Spare Underwear

An unexpected period or leak can happen. Clean underwear can make the situation much easier to manage.

### Tissues

Useful as an everyday personal-hygiene item.

### Disposal Bags

Small bags can be useful for securely wrapping used products where appropriate disposal facilities are not immediately available.

### A Small Calendar or Period Tracker

Once menstruation begins, recording the first day of each period can help her gradually understand her menstrual pattern.

### Personalise It

Let her choose the pouch.

She might prefer something colourful, simple, fashionable or discreet.

Giving her ownership can help the kit feel like normal personal care rather than an emergency pack.

### Check It Together

Period kits need restocking.

Create a simple habit of checking supplies periodically without making it intrusive.

### Yazz® Parent Tip

Consider keeping period essentials in more than one location:

School bag. Home. Travel bag.

Preparation creates options.
