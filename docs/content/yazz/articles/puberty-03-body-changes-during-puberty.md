### What's Happening to My Body?

One day your clothes fit perfectly. A few months later, things may feel different.

Puberty brings physical changes because your body is developing.

### Breast Development

Breasts may begin developing during puberty. One breast may sometimes appear to develop differently or at a different pace from the other.

Bodies vary, and development happens gradually.

### Body Hair

Hair may begin growing:
- Under your arms
- Around your pubic area
- More noticeably on other parts of your body

This is a normal part of puberty.

### Growing Taller

You may experience a period of faster growth.

Your height, weight and body proportions can change as you develop.

### Skin Changes

Hormonal changes can cause the skin to produce more oil. This can contribute to spots or acne.

A simple, gentle skincare routine can help you care for your skin.

### Sweating and Body Odour

You may begin sweating more, particularly under your arms.

Regular bathing, clean clothing and appropriate personal-care products can help manage body odour.

### Vaginal Discharge

You may begin noticing clear or whitish discharge in your underwear. This can be a normal part of puberty.

Learn more about this in the next article.

### Yazz® Tip Just 4 You

Your body isn't supposed to look exactly like anybody else's.

Give yourself time to grow.
