### Help Her Like Who She's Becoming, Not Chase a "Perfect" Body

Puberty can make young people extremely aware of their appearance.

Bodies develop.

Skin changes.

Height changes.

Weight and body proportions change.

Friends develop at different rates.

And social media can create unrealistic comparisons.

Parents and caregivers have an important role in creating a healthier environment.

### Be Careful with Body Comments

Avoid teasing or repeatedly commenting on:
- Weight
- Breast development
- Height
- Skin
- Body shape
- How quickly or slowly she is developing
- Even comments intended as jokes can affect confidence.

### Praise More Than Appearance

Notice her:
- Kindness
- Creativity
- Curiosity
- Courage
- Responsibility
- Effort
- Problem-solving
- Talents
- Leadership
- Academic progress
- Help her understand that her value isn't determined by how she looks.

### Watch Your Own Language

Young people listen to how adults talk about their own bodies and other people's bodies.

Model respectful language.

### Teach Social Media Awareness

Help her understand that online images may be carefully selected, posed, filtered or edited.

She shouldn't measure normal development against a curated screen.

### Yazz® Parent Tip

Instead of teaching her to ask:

"Am I pretty enough?"

help her develop a much bigger question:

"Who am I becoming?"
