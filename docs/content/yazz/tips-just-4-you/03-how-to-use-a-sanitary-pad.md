If you've never used a sanitary pad before, it may seem unfamiliar at first.

A sanitary pad is worn inside your underwear to absorb menstrual flow.

### Using Your Yazz® Pad
1. Wash and dry your hands. 2. Remove the pad from its individual wrapper. 3. Peel away the protective backing. 4. Position the pad securely in the centre of your underwear. 5. If your Yazz® pad has wings, fold them around the sides of your underwear to help keep the pad in place. 6. Change your pad regularly and whenever it feels full,

uncomfortable or needs replacing. 7. Wrap the used pad securely before placing it in an appropriate waste bin. 8. Wash your hands afterwards. Never flush sanitary pads down the toilet.

Always follow the usage and disposal instructions displayed on your Yazz® packaging.
