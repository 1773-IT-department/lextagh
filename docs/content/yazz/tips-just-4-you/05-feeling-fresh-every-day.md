### Freshness Begins with Good Personal Hygiene

Taking care of yourself during your period doesn't need to be complicated.

Simple hygiene habits can help you stay comfortable and confident.

### Tips Just 4 You
- Change regularly. Avoid wearing the same sanitary pad for longer than necessary. Change it according to your flow, comfort needs and product directions.
- Wash your hands. Wash your hands before and after changing your sanitary pad.
- Keep clean. Regular bathing and appropriate personal hygiene are important throughout your cycle.
- Wear clean underwear. Fresh, comfortable underwear can help you feel more comfortable throughout your day.
- Carry your essentials. Keeping an extra pad with you can make unexpected situations easier to manage.
- Dispose responsibly.

Wrap used sanitary pads and place them in an appropriate waste bin. Do not flush them.
