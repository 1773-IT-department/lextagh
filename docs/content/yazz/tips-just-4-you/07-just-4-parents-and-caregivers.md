Helping Her Understand. Helping Her Feel Prepared.

Talking about puberty and menstruation before a girl's first period can help remove fear, embarrassment and misinformation.

Yazz® wants to help parents and caregivers make those conversations easier.

### Start With a Conversation

You don't need to turn menstruation into one big, uncomfortable conversation.

Create opportunities for simple, age-appropriate discussions about growing up, body changes, hygiene and periods.

Let her know she can ask questions.

Most importantly, help her understand that menstruation is normal.

### Just 4 Parents & Caregivers

Helping Her Understand. Helping Her Prepare. Helping Her Grow with Confidence.

Growing up comes with questions.

For a young girl approaching puberty, those questions may be about her changing body, her first period, personal hygiene, emotions, friendships and whether what she is experiencing is "normal."

Having a supportive adult who is willing to listen and provide accurate information can make a meaningful difference.

Just 4 Parents & Caregivers is part of the Yazz® Tips Just 4 You educational platform, created to help parents, guardians and caregivers approach puberty and menstruation with openness, sensitivity and practical information.

You don't need to know every answer.

Sometimes the most important thing you can tell her is:

"You can always talk to me."
