### Give Her Information Before She Has to Search for It Alone

One of the most powerful things a parent or caregiver can give a young person approaching puberty isn't a sanitary pad.

It's an environment where she knows:

I can ask questions.

I don't have to be ashamed of my period.

My changing body deserves care and respect.

If something worries me, someone will listen.

Then give her the practical tools she needs too.

Prepare the period kit.

Teach her how to use her sanitary pad.

Talk about school.

Talk about hygiene.

Correct the myths.

Listen to her concerns.

And keep the conversation going.
