### TIPS JUST 4 YOU

Periods. Puberty. Protection. Confidence. Just 4 You.

Welcome to Tips Just 4 You the Yazz® space created to help you understand your period, care for yourself, choose the right menstrual protection and feel more confident through every stage of your journey.

Whether you're waiting for your first period, already learning about your cycle, looking for everyday hygiene tips, or you're a parent helping a young person through puberty, we've created something Just 4 You.

Because the more you understand your body, the more confidently you can care for yourself.

Know Your Body. Know Your Flow. Own Your Day.
