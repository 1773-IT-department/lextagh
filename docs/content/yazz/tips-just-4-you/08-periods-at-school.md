Be Prepared. Stay Confident. Keep Learning.

Getting your period during school shouldn't mean your school day has to end.

### Before School

Keep a period kit in your school bag.

### During School

If you need help, speak with a teacher, school nurse or another trusted adult.

### After Changing Your Pad

Wrap and dispose of your used sanitary pad appropriately and wash your hands.

### Keep Track

Tracking your menstrual cycle may help you estimate when your next period could arrive, although cycles can sometimes vary.

Your period is part of your life; it doesn't define what you can achieve.
