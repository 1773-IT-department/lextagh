### Which Pad Is Right 4 Me?

Your flow can change during your period. What feels suitable during the first few days may be different from what you prefer toward the end of your period.

When choosing a pad, think about: The Yazz® website can we feature a simple interactive selector: if this could be inserted in the system to be created to indicate which pad to consider during what time it will go a long way
- Your Flow Is today lighter, moderate or heavier than usual?
- Your Day Are you going to school, work, travelling, relaxing at home or staying active?
- Your Comfort Choose protection that feels comfortable and secure for you.
- Day or Night You may prefer different coverage depending on whether you're going about your day or sleeping.
