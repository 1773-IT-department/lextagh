## Yazz Pads & Panty Liners | EMPOWERED PERIODS, DIGNITY PROJECT

Every period deserves dignity. Through menstrual education, product-access partnerships and school/community conversations, Empowered Periods aims to help girls and women manage menstruation with knowledge, confidence and support. CTA: Propose a School / Partner with Us.

## Yazz Feminine Care | CERVICAL CANCER AWARENESS

Knowledge can support prevention and earlier action. Yazz seeks to amplify professionally guided education on cervical health, screening and HPV vaccination through suitable health-sector partnerships. CTA: Explore Awareness / Propose a Partnership.

## Yazz Tissues | FOREST PROJECT, POWERED BY LEXTA

Care for our homes should go hand in hand with care for the environment. The Lexta Forest Project is a proposed tree-planting and environmental-education platform linking everyday paper use with responsible stewardship. CTA: Support a Green Activity.

## Yazz Detergent | CLEAN HANDS, GREEN LAND

Clean homes and a cleaner environment belong together. This proposed programme focuses on practical hygiene education, responsible packaging disposal and community clean-up partnerships. CTA: Join an Activation.

## Yazz Baby Wipes & Nappies | THE "SAFE BOTTOMS" INITIATIVE

Good baby hygiene begins with information and access. Safe Bottoms is designed to support caregiver education, responsible diaper and wipe disposal and community hygiene partnerships. CTA: Partner With Us.

## Yazz Toothpaste & Toothbrushes | THE "BRIGHT SMILES" SCHOOL TOUR

Healthy smiles begin with everyday habits. The Bright Smiles School Tour proposes age-appropriate oral-hygiene education and partnerships with qualified dental professionals and schools. CTA: Invite the Tour.
