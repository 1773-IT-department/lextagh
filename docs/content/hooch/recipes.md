**Make Every Morning a Hooch® Moment!**

Breakfast doesn't have to be complicated to be enjoyable. Discover simple ways to add colour, variety and excitement to your Hooch® breakfast.

## The Classic Hooch® Bowl: Simple. Crunchy. Delicious.

Pour your Hooch® Cornflakes into a bowl, add milk and enjoy. Include sliced banana for a naturally sweet addition.

Preparation: approximately 3 minutes.

## The Frosted Fruit Delight: A Sweet Start with a Fruity Twist.

Add Hooch® Frosted Flakes to a bowl and top with fresh strawberries, banana or other seasonal fruit. Serve with milk.

Preparation: approximately 5 minutes.

## Hooch® Yoghurt Crunch: Crunch Meets Creamy Goodness.

Layer plain yoghurt, fresh fruit and Hooch® Cornflakes in a glass. Serve immediately to enjoy the crunchy texture.

Preparation: approximately 5 minutes.

## The Hooch® After-School Crunch: A Little Crunch for Snack Time.

Serve a small portion of Hooch® alongside sliced fruit for an easy snack after school or during a family break.

Preparation: approximately 3 minutes.
