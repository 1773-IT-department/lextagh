### Recipes & Breakfast Inspiration

- **Breakfast Crunch Bowl:** Serve Hooch cornflakes with milk or an appropriate alternative and sliced banana. Adjust to dietary needs and product labels.
- **Fruit & Crunch Cup:** Layer plain yoghurt, chopped seasonal fruit and Hooch cornflakes just before serving for crunch.
- **After-School Crunch Mix:** Combine cereal with suitable dried fruit and seeds for age-appropriate snacking; check allergies.
- **Celebration Fruit Cups:** Arrange diced fruit and yoghurt in small cups, topping with cereal immediately before serving.
- **Crunchy Dessert Topper:** Sprinkle a small amount of cereal over a chilled fruit dessert just before serving.

HOOCH® FOODS. Good Nutrition, Brighter Tomorrow.

The Hooch® website will position the brand as a cheerful, family-focused breakfast companion, highlighting its two products: Hooch® Cornflakes (500g) and Hooch® Frosted Flakes (375g).

### Tab: About Hooch®

**Our Story: Bringing Families Together, One Breakfast at a Time**

Hooch® is a breakfast cereal brand from Lexta Ghana Limited, created to bring delicious, convenient and enjoyable breakfast options to families.

We understand that mornings can be busy. From preparing children for school to getting ready for work, families need breakfast choices that are easy to serve and enjoyable to eat.

That's why Hooch® offers two cereal favorites in convenient individual portions: the classic crunch of Hooch® Cornflakes and the sweet delight of Hooch® Frosted Flakes.

Our goal is simple: to make breakfast a moment families can look forward to.

**Our Brand Promise: Good Nutrition, Brighter Tomorrow.**

We believe that everyday food choices contribute to healthier routines and enjoyable family experiences.

Through convenience, enjoyable flavors and a commitment to continuous product improvement, Hooch® seeks to become a trusted part of family breakfast traditions.

**Our Values**

- **Family:** Creating enjoyable breakfast moments that bring people together.
- **Convenience:** Making busy mornings easier with individually portioned cereal packs.
- **Quality:** Maintaining a commitment to dependable products and consumer satisfaction.
- **Care:** Listening to families and responding to their everyday needs.

**Proudly a Lexta Ghana Limited Brand**

Hooch® forms part of Lexta Ghana Limited's growing portfolio of Ghanaian consumer brands, alongside Yazz® & Linzzy®.
