## Product FAQs

**Can I eat Hooch® without milk?** Yes. Hooch® cereals can also be enjoyed as a crunchy snack.

**Can children enjoy Hooch®?** Hooch® may be included in age-appropriate diets. Parents should consider the child's age, dietary needs, allergies and the product's nutrition label.

**How should I store my Hooch®?** Keep the product in a cool, dry place and follow the storage instructions on the packaging.

**What is the difference between Cornflakes and Frosted Flakes?** Hooch® Cornflakes offers a classic cereal taste, while Frosted Flakes provides a sweeter, frosted alternative.

## Beyond the Bowl: Get Creative with Hooch®!

Who says Hooch® is only for breakfast? From crunchy snacks to creative desserts and homemade treats, there are many delicious ways to enjoy Hooch® Cornflakes and Hooch® Frosted Flakes. Explore our frequently asked questions for inspiration in the kitchen!

1. **Can I use Hooch® Cornflakes for baking?** Absolutely! Hooch® Cornflakes can add a crunchy texture to homemade cookies, cereal bars and other baked treats. Try lightly crushing the flakes and mixing them into your favorite baking recipes.
2. **Can I make cookies with Hooch® Frosted Flakes?** Yes! The sweet frosted flavor makes Hooch® Frosted Flakes a fun addition to cookies and dessert recipes. Fold them into cookie dough or use crushed flakes as a topping. Remember to adjust other sweet ingredients to suit your taste.
3. **Can I use Hooch® Cornflakes as a crunchy coating for food?** Yes. Crush Hooch® Cornflakes and use them as a coating for oven-baked chicken, fish or vegetables. Season to your preference and cook thoroughly using appropriate food-safety temperatures.
4. **Can I make homemade cereal bars with Hooch®?** Certainly! Combine Hooch® Cornflakes with a suitable binding mixture, press into a lined tray and chill or bake according to your chosen recipe. Cut into portions for a convenient homemade snack.
5. **Which Hooch® product is better for savoury recipes?** Hooch® Cornflakes are generally the more versatile option for savoury cooking because of their classic flavor. Hooch® Frosted Flakes are better suited to recipes where a sweeter taste is desired.
6. **Can I use Hooch® as a topping for yoghurt or ice cream?** Of course! Sprinkle Hooch® Cornflakes or Frosted Flakes over yoghurt, fruit bowls or ice cream just before serving for extra crunch.
7. **Can children help prepare Hooch® recipes?** Yes! Children can help with age-appropriate tasks such as pouring cereal, mixing cool ingredients and decorating treats. An adult should supervise activities involving ovens, hot surfaces or sharp utensils.
8. **How can I keep Hooch® crunchy when using it in recipes?** Add the cereal shortly before serving when making yoghurt bowls, fruit desserts or other moist dishes. Store unused cereal in its original packaging, properly sealed, in a cool, dry place.
