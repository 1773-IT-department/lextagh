### Developer navigation and content rules (Lexta)

- All website pages should include breadcrumb navigation, related-category links, clear CTA buttons, accessible mobile menus and footer legal links.
- The agreed brand-page maximum is six pages per brand; use on-page tabs, anchored sections and article collections where feasible.
- All product cards must pull from the shared Lexta catalogue using unique SKU/product codes, with brand, category, variant, pack size, approved description, image and availability status.
- Use enquiry / Where to Buy rather than a functioning checkout until e-commerce is separately commissioned.
- Privacy: minimise personal information, obtain specific consent, restrict file attachments, publish privacy/retention notices and test email routing without exposing customer information.
- Use only approved claims and accurate certifications; QA must verify sanitary-pad specs, diaper weight ranges, wipes ingredients, toothpaste claims and Hooch nutrition/allergens. These will be worked on and shared separately.

### About Lexta

Lexta Ghana Limited is a Ghanaian consumer-goods company committed to making dependable everyday products more accessible to households. Across Yazz, Linzzy and Hooch, we bring care, innovation and value together with an ambition to serve families throughout Africa. Our mission is to combine quality, innovation and affordability in products that meet family needs and deliver value to stakeholders. Our vision is to be the preferred choice for family-care products across Africa.

Our care extends beyond our products. Through education, hygiene awareness, environmental responsibility and community partnerships, Lexta seeks to help create healthier, more informed and more sustainable communities.
