About Lexta Ghana
Founded on the principles of care, quality, and innovation, Lexta Ghana Limited is a premier consumer goods company dedicated to enhancing the everyday lives of families across Ghana and beyond. We manufacture and distribute high-quality home and personal care products designed to deliver exceptional value without compromising on standards. From robust household cleaners to gentle personal hygiene essentials, Lexta is a brand built on trust.

Our History, Vision and Mission

Our History
We combine quality, innovation and affordability to provide care products that meet the needs of the family and give our stakeholders maximum returns
To be the preferred choice for family care products across Africa
Driven by a vision to improve everyday living through quality and affordable consumer products, Lexta Ghana Limited was established in 2002 as a proudly Ghanaian-owned company.
What began as a supplier of home care products has grown into one of the most trusted and respected consumer goods companies in the region. Over the years, Lexta has expanded its footprint across the Home Care, Personal Care, and Food categories, consistently delivering products that meet the evolving needs of consumers and families.
Today, our presence extends beyond Ghana into the wider West African sub-region, including a wholly-owned subsidiary in Monrovia, Liberia, with ongoing plans to strengthen our regional presence through strategic expansion into additional markets.
Our growth and success have been built on a foundation of innovation, investment in people, research and development, and an unwavering commitment to quality. These principles continue to drive our ambition of becoming the preferred choice for consumers across Africa.
At the heart of our portfolio are our flagship brands YAZZ, HOOCH, ROXX and LINZZY each carefully developed to provide dependable, high-quality solutions that enhance everyday life.
At Lexta, we believe that care should be accessible to every home. Guided by our promise, “Caring for Your Everyday,” we remain committed to making quality products affordable and available to the communities we serve.
Together with our trusted brands YAZZ, HOOCH, and LINZZY we continue our mission of delivering quality, affordability, and care to millions of consumers across Africa, every day.
"What is Care Without Quality?"
At Lexta, our operational philosophy is anchored on a single, unyielding conviction: true care requires uncompromising standards. Since our inception, we have evolved from a localized supplier into a competitive pan-African enterprise. We achieve this by investing heavily in rigorous quality control, agile manufacturing infrastructure, and sub-regional distribution networks that keep our promises affordable, accessible, flawless and with care.

## Lexta Ghana Limited spent time with the girls of Unity high JHS in Kotobabi in commemoration of the internal women’s day. 2019-03-08T11:58:00

Source: https://lextagh.com/2019/03/08/lexta-ghana-limited-spent-time-with-the-girls-of-unity-high-jhs-in-kotobabi-in-commemoration-of-the-internal-womens-day/

## Lexta Teen Care Campaign 2017-05-30T11:49:00

Source: https://lextagh.com/2017/05/30/lexta-teen-care-campaign/


As our flagship CSR project, the Lexta Teen Care Campaign aims at teaching students in Senior High Schools (SHS) how to properly cultivate good personal care and general hygiene habits in their adolescent years, a crucial phase in their development.
Keeping clean is an important part of staying healthy and safe from diseases and infections. In the adolescent stage, cleanliness cannot be overemphasized owing to the numerous changes that occur as one begins adulthood.
As a Company that cares, Lexta believes there is the need for more education on the importance of personal hygiene for adolescence and this led to the birth of the Lexta Teen Care Campaign.
The Campaign which was launched in February 2017 is set to educate SHS students across the country on cervical cancer, menstrual hygiene and oral care. A number of clean-up competitions will be executed to keep our school environments neat and tidy.
Cervical Cancer: Available statistics indicate that it is the leading cause of cancer death for women in 40 of the 48 countries of sub-Saharan Africa. It affects younger age groups as a result of early sexual activity, multiple sexual partners, and exposure to other sexually transmitted infections such as HIV. Thus, educating Ghanaian teenagers – both boys and girls on the dangers of cervical cancer would serve as a preventive measure aimed at reducing the infection rate of the disease in the country.
Menstrual Hygiene: According to WASH United (2014), the majority of the female population who are of reproductive age in Africa do not have access to clean and safe sanitary products or a clean and private space to change their menstrual cloths or pads. Poor protection and inadequate washing facilities may increase susceptibility to infection of their reproductive tract, with the odour of menstrual blood putting girls at risk of being stigmatized. The education on menstrual hygiene is our contribution to raising awareness about the importance of practicing good menstrual hygiene.
Oral Care: The World Health Organization (WHO) describes oral health as a key indicator of overall health due to its systemic and reciprocal links with general health. The mouth which is made up of the teeth, masticatory muscles, tongue, mucosal soft tissues, and salivary glands, is the body’s main portal for nutrition. It is regarded as the starting point of the body’s defense system and immunity thus, when oral health is compromised by disease or injury, general health suffers. A 2012 FDI Report indicated that across the world, 60–90% of school children and nearly 100% of adults have dental cavities. Being a producer of oral care products, we recognize the health and social benefits of practicing good oral hygiene which is what necessitated this component under the Lexta Teen Care Campaign.
Clean-up competitions: Poor sanitation affects public health and as a nation battling with issues of sanitation, we found it imperative to collaborate with schools by participating in their weekly and/or monthly inspections as a means of drumming home the importance of keeping their surroundings clean which goes a long way to reduce the incidence of sanitation-related diseases such as cholera, diarrhoea, dysentery, hepatitis A and typhoid.
