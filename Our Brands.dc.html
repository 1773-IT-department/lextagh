Our Brands

Three names. One standard.

Behind Yazz, Linzzy and Hooch is a single company, a single factory and a single refusal to trade quality for price. Different aisles. Different needs. Same promise.

Jump to Yazz Linzzy Hooch What holds them together →

Most Ghanaians know our brands better than they know us. That suits us.

Yazz has been on shelves since the early days of the company. Linzzy speaks to a newer generation. Hooch sits on the breakfast table. Each was built for a specific job in a specific home, and each is made by Lexta in Accra, to the same standard, at a price the household in front of us can actually pay.

Here is what each of them does.

The champion of the everyday

Yazz

Yazz is our flagship, and our widest range. Personal care, oral care, tissue and cleaning, built for people who need things to simply work. Named Ghana's most preferred sanitary and hygiene brand at the 2022 Home Builders Awards, it remains the brand most Ghanaians reach for without thinking about it. That is the highest compliment a product like this can earn.

What we make it for

Confidence. The kind that comes from not having to think about the thing you are wearing, the shirt you just washed, or the smile you just gave someone.

What we refuse to do

Cut the materials. Yazz is priced for the household budget, not built down to it.

The Yazz range
Sanitary pads and panty liners

Winged, high absorbency pads engineered for security and comfort through a full day. Available across regular, heavy flow and extra long formats, with panty liners for everyday freshness.

Toothpaste

The new Yazz range comes in three variants: Charcoal, Herbal and Fluoride. Formulated with calcium carbonate, fluoride and sodium to strengthen enamel and guard against plaque, cavities, bleeding gums and bad breath. The Herbal variant carries a blend of herbs that soothes the gums.

Toothbrushes

Designed to pair with the range, built to last longer than the month you bought them for.

Washing powder

Active enzymes formulated to lift the toughest stains, with perfumed blue speckles that leave clothes smelling fresh. Clinically tested to be gentle on hands. Tough on stains, kind on fabric.

Baby wipes

Alcohol free, gently scented wipes that clean and care for delicate skin, leaving it fresh and smooth. Available in three variants.

Baby diapers

Absorbent, secure and comfortable, for the long nights and the longer days.

Tissue and kitchen towels

Pocket tissues, toilet rolls, and Mama Yazz, our two ply kitchen towel, named for the number of jobs it ends up doing in a Ghanaian kitchen.

Sophisticated protection

Linzzy

Linzzy is our answer to a generation that expects more from a personal care product than absorption. Cleaner design, clinical performance, and a quieter kind of confidence. Where Yazz is the trusted everyday, Linzzy is precise, modern and unmistakably deliberate.

Built on engineering, not marketing

Structured absorption layers and anti leak barriers designed around how the product is actually worn, not how it photographs.

Built for sensitive skin

Fragrance free formulations across the baby range, tested to guard against irritation rather than mask it.

The Linzzy range
Sanitary napkins

A full portfolio including Cool Mint, Heavy Flow and Extra Long, each with clinical anti leak barriers and structured absorption designed for a specific need rather than an average one.

Baby wipes

Ultra mild, fragrance free wipes in 80 piece packs, formulated for sensitive skin and built to hold up to the job.

Baby care

A growing range of everyday essentials for the youngest members of the household.

Fuelled by integrity

Hooch

Breakfast decides the morning. Hooch is built for mornings that have to last: pure grains, vitamin fortified, low in fat, and formulated to avoid the sugar crash that most cereals build in and then quietly rely on. For families, for students, for anyone whose day starts early and ends late.

Fortified, not just sweetened

Essential vitamins added for real metabolic value, not a claim on the front of the box.

Made for the long morning

A grain profile designed to release energy steadily, so ten o'clock feels like nine.

The Hooch range
Super Crunchy Corn Flakes

Traditional, crisp, sun ripened corn flakes toasted for a crunch that survives the milk, fortified with essential vitamins.

Frosted Flakes

A measured balance of sweetness and high energy grain, low in fat, made for active mornings and small people with strong opinions.

Choco Flakes

The same fortified grain base, finished with cocoa. Available in 375g.

What holds them together

Three brands, one factory, one standard

The names differ because the needs differ. What does not change is what sits behind them.

Every Yazz pad, every Linzzy wipe, and every box of Hooch comes off lines that run to international hygiene and regulatory benchmarks, with batch level quality checks before anything leaves our facility in Accra. We formulate against how Ghanaian families actually live, not against a laboratory assumption, and we hold the price because affordability is part of the product, not a discount applied afterwards.

That is the whole idea. Care, at a standard, that people can actually reach.
