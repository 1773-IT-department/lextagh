People and Culture

Life at Lexta

We make products that hundreds of thousands of Ghanaian households depend on. That work is done by people, and how we treat them shows up in what leaves the factory.

Why our people come first

A company grows at the speed of its team

Our greatest asset is not our machinery or our distribution network. It is the several hundred people who run them.

We have never believed that a company can outgrow the people inside it. So we build for the long term: an environment where your wellbeing is taken seriously, your voice carries weight regardless of your title, and your development is treated as a shared investment rather than a personal problem.

Some of the people who joined Lexta in its early years are still here, in bigger roles than the ones they started in. That is not an accident, and it is the outcome we are most proud of.

The Lexta DNA

What makes a Lexta team member

Our culture rests on five pillars. They are not posters on a wall. They are how decisions actually get made here.

01
Entrepreneurship

We hire people who take ownership. If you see something that could work better, we would rather you fix it than file it. You are trusted to take initiative, think creatively and run your work like it is your own business.

02
Team cohesion

We win as one company, not as competing departments. Production talks to sales. Sales talks to marketing. Nobody here gets to succeed while the person next to them fails.

03
Growth

Your progress is our progress. Through training, mentorship and clear career pathways, we invest in where you are going, not just what you are doing now.

04
Drive

We are a company that moves. We bring energy to the work, we push at the edges of what is possible, and we do not wait to be told twice.

05
Results

We set high standards, we measure honestly, and we celebrate together when we hit them. Effort matters. Outcomes matter more.

Where you could work

More roles than most people expect

Lexta is a manufacturer, a marketer and a distributor at once. That means the range of work here is far wider than a consumer goods company usually looks from the outside.

Manufacturing and production

Machine operators, line supervisors, production planners and engineers. The heart of the business, running to international hygiene and regulatory standards.

Quality assurance

The people who decide whether a batch leaves the building. Rigorous, uncompromising, and the reason our brands hold their reputation.

Sales and distribution

Field representatives, area managers and key account teams working across Ghana and into Liberia, moving our brands from our floor to the shelves people actually shop from.

Marketing and brand

Brand managers and marketing executives building Yazz, Linzzy and Hooch, and running the CSR campaigns that put us in schools and clinics nationwide.

Supply chain and logistics

Warehousing, fleet, procurement and planning. The discipline that keeps a product available in Tamale on the same terms as in Accra.

Finance, HR and administration

The functions that let everything else run, staffed by people who understand a manufacturing business rather than a generic one.

Growth

We would rather promote you than replace you

Our first instinct when a role opens is to look inside the building. We invest in continuous training, both technical and professional, and we pair newer team members with people who have done the job long enough to teach it properly.

Career paths here are discussed openly, not guessed at. You should know what the next role looks like, what it takes to get there, and who is helping you.

Structured onboarding

A proper first few weeks, with someone accountable for showing you how things actually run here.

Mentorship

Experienced colleagues who are expected to develop the people around them, not just deliver their own numbers.

Ongoing training

Technical certification, safety and compliance and professional development, kept current rather than filed away.

Diversity and inclusion

Innovation needs different perspectives in the room

We build products for every kind of Ghanaian household, which means we need a workforce that actually reflects them.

We champion equal opportunity across gender, background and physical ability, and we hold ourselves to it in hiring, in promotion and in how meetings are run. We do not merely accept difference. We rely on it. It makes our products better, our decisions sharper and our company a place more people want to stay.

Beyond the job

Work that reaches past the factory gate

Our teams do not only make and sell products. They run the outreach that takes those products where they are needed most.

Our people have delivered free cervical cancer screening nationwide alongside the Medical Women Association of Ghana, including in Tamale and at Accra Psychiatric Hospital. They have run menstrual health education in schools across the country and campaigned against period poverty. They have partnered with the Ghana Education Service and the Ghana Police Service on programmes for students.

If that kind of work matters to you, there is room for it here.

How we hire

What to expect from us

We try to keep our process clear and quick, and we tell you where you stand.

1
Apply

Submit your application online. You will get a confirmation, not silence.

2
Screening

We review every application. Shortlisted candidates hear from us within a defined window.

3
Interview

A conversation with the hiring manager and the team you would work with. We will tell you what we are looking for.

4
Assessment

For some roles, a practical exercise relevant to the actual job. Nothing artificial.

5
Offer and onboarding

A clear offer, a proper start, and a structured first few weeks.
