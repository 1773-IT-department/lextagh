Draft one
Please read this first

This is a first draft, and we want it marked up

We have designed a complete first version of a new website for Lexta Ghana. It is not a set of slides describing a website; it is real, working pages that you can click through on a phone or a laptop today. The pages that follow show each one, with a short note on what it does and why.

Everything you see is a proposal. The headlines, the photographs, the colours, the order of the sections and the words themselves are all open to change.

What is in the draft
Twelve pages: home, about, brands, people and culture, community work, news, careers, partnerships, contact, and the supporting pages every company site needs.
What is still missing
Your photographs. Several images in the draft are stand ins, waiting for the real product shots, factory pictures and event photography from Lexta.
How to give feedback
Write on this document, send us a voice note, or walk through the pages with us on a call. Whatever is easiest. Page numbers make it simple to point at something.
What we need to know
Whether the overall direction feels like Lexta. If it does not, say so plainly and we will show you a different one. Nothing here is precious to us.
A note on the screenshots. Each screenshot shows the top of a page, which is the part most visitors see. The pages continue below what is pictured, and we are happy to walk through the full length of any of them with you.

The direction

How the draft looks, and why

We kept the Lexta blue rather than inventing a new identity. What changes is how it is used: more white space, larger photographs of real products and real people, and shorter writing.

Colour
Lexta blue
Buttons and links
Deep navy
Headings, dark panels
Soft blue white
Section backgrounds
Type
Caring for your everyday
One typeface throughout, heavy for headlines and light for reading. Nothing decorative, so the products stay the loudest thing on the page.
Photography does the selling
Ghanaian homes, Ghanaian faces and the factory floor, shot large. Where we do not yet have a picture, the design leaves a marked space so you can see exactly what is needed.
Written for a phone
Short sentences, no jargon. Most visitors will read the site on a phone, standing up, in under a minute. The draft assumes that.
The same frame on every page
The menu, the two buttons in the corner and the footer never move, so a visitor always knows where they are and how to reach you.
Built to be changed
Each page is made of blocks that can be reordered, removed or repeated. Adjusting the draft after your feedback is quick, so please do not hold back.

Page by page

Home

Website page 1 of 12

The first ten seconds. A rotating set of full width pictures introduces the company, the brands and the factory, each with a single line of text and one button.

Who Lexta is, stated immediately
"Proudly Ghanaian since 2002" sits above the headline, so the age and origin of the company are known before anyone scrolls.
The brands appear early
Yazz, Linzzy and Hooch are shown with their packaging on the first screen, because most people know the packet before they know the company.
Two clear invitations
"Contact Us" and "Partner With Us" sit in the top corner of every page, so a serious enquiry is never more than one click away.
Open question for you
Which three messages should the rotating pictures carry? We have suggested history, brands and manufacturing.

About Us

Website page 2 of 12

Opens on the production line with the line "We make what we sell, under our own roof", which is the single most important fact about Lexta, said plainly.

A welcome from the founder
A short personal message directly under the opening picture, which gives the company a face instead of a logo.
The story from 2002 onwards
A simple timeline of how Lexta grew from a home care supplier into a manufacturer serving the sub region.
Values and standards
What the company holds itself to, and the quality checks the factory runs, written for a general reader rather than an auditor.

Our Brands

Website page 3 of 12

"Three names. One standard." Yazz, Linzzy and Hooch each get their own full section, with quick links at the top so a visitor can jump straight to the one they came for.

One section per brand
What the brand is for, who buys it, and the full range of packs and sizes, shown at a size where the packaging is recognisable.

People & Culture

Website page 4 of 12

"Life at Lexta". The page about the workforce behind the products: the teams, the training, safety on the floor, and what Lexta expects of itself as an employer.

Layout of the page
1
Opening picture of the team on the factory floor, with the line "Life at Lexta" and two buttons: view current openings, and learn about our work.
2
Why people come first, a short piece on how the company treats the people who make the products.
3
The kinds of work Lexta offers: production, quality, sales and distribution, administration, so a visitor can picture where they might fit.
4
Training, safety and benefits, then a direct link through to the current openings on the careers page.
Why the page earns its place
Buyers and partners judge a manufacturer partly on how it treats its workforce. This page answers that quietly, with pictures rather than claims.
What we need from Lexta
Photographs of staff at work, and permission from the people pictured. Two or three short quotes from long serving employees would lift the page considerably.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Website page 5 of 12

"Care that leaves the shelf". School visits, menstrual health education, screening events and donations, told through photographs from the events themselves.

Programmes, not slogans
Each campaign is named and dated, with where it happened and who it reached, so the work reads as real activity rather than general goodwill.
The partners are shown
The health services, associations and hospitals Lexta works with appear with their logos, which lends the programme weight.
Tied back to the products
The page argues that a company making sanitary pads should be working on period poverty. The link between what Lexta sells and what it supports is made explicit.
What we need from Lexta
Event photographs at full resolution, with dates, locations and the number of people reached.

Page by page

News

Website page 6 of 12

Product launches, company announcements and press coverage, newest first, with the most recent story given the full width of the page.

Quick to add to
A single item takes a few minutes to publish: a picture, a headline, a date and a short paragraph. The rest of the page arranges itself.
Press coverage counts
Television and newspaper mentions can be listed alongside Lexta's own announcements, which is often more persuasive than either alone.
It keeps the site alive
A site that has not changed in two years tells its own story. One or two items a month is enough to avoid that.
What we need from Lexta
One named person who will send us news as it happens. We will write it up and publish it.

Careers

Current openings, what it is like to work at Lexta, and a proper application route so that applications arrive complete and in one place.

Roles listed clearly
Title, department, location and closing date, so candidates can see at a glance whether a role is worth their time.

Address, telephone, email and a map, with the enquiry sent to the right department instead of a single general mailbox.

The three supporting pages

Quieter pages that nobody praises, but whose absence is noticed by buyers, regulators and careful customers. They are written in plain language rather than legal boilerplate.

Frequently Asked Questions
Website page 10 of 12
The questions Lexta already answers by telephone every week: where to buy, whether products are made locally, how to become a distributor, whether the ingredients are safe. Answering them once here saves your team time and reassures the visitor at the moment of doubt.
Privacy Policy
Website page 11 of 12
What information the site collects when someone fills in a form, what it is used for, and how long it is kept. Required in practice by Ghana's data protection rules, and expected by any serious partner.
Terms and Conditions
Website page 12 of 12
The terms on which the site is used, including how product information and images may be reproduced by retailers and the press.
These three pages are drafted, but they should be read by whoever advises Lexta on legal matters before the site goes live. We will make any changes they ask for.

After launch

Keeping the site alive once it is live

Most websites decline quietly. Nobody updates them, nothing is backed up, and two years later they are worse than what came before. We propose a standing arrangement so that does not happen here.

Hosting and domain management
We keep the site online and the lextagh.com address renewed on time, so it never lapses by accident.
Security updates and backups
Regular updates and copies kept off site, so the whole site can be restored quickly if anything goes wrong.
Monthly content and news updates
Lexta sends us the news; we write it up, add the photographs and publish it. No technical work on your side.
Product and brand page updates
New packs, new sizes, refreshed packaging and discontinued lines kept accurate across the brand pages.
A monthly report in plain language
One page each month: how many people visited, which pages they read, how many enquiries came in, and what we suggest changing next. No jargon and no dashboards to log into.
Timeline and cost are deliberately left out of this draft. We would rather agree with you what the site should contain, then put a schedule and a figure against exactly that, no more and no less.

What happens after you have read this

Four steps. The first two cost Lexta nothing but time.

1
Walk through the draft together
One hour, in your office or on a call, going through every page on screen. We want to hear what is wrong at least as much as what is right.
2
We revise the draft
Your corrections go in and we show you a second version. If the direction itself is wrong, we will design an alternative rather than defend this one.
3
Content and photography
Lexta provides product shots, factory and staff pictures, and the company details flagged on each page. We write and lay out the rest.
4
Test, approve and go live
You test the finished site on a private link, on real phones and computers. When you are satisfied, we agree a launch date and move it onto lextagh.com with the support arrangement in place.
The only thing we are asking for today
A date for step one, and your honest reaction to the draft. Everything else follows from that conversation.
