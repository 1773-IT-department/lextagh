What you get

The terms behind the job title

Details vary by role and level, and we set them out plainly in the offer rather than after you sign.

Health cover

Medical cover for you, extended to dependants for permanent staff, plus on-site first aid and periodic screening.

Pension and statutory benefits

SSNIT tier one and tier two contributions handled properly and on time, with clear payslips every month.

Training budget

Technical certification, safety and compliance training and professional development, funded rather than encouraged.

Performance bonus

Annual review with a bonus tied to targets you can see and influence, not a number decided in a closed room.

Staff product allocation

A monthly allocation of Yazz, Linzzy, Crox and Softcare products for your household.

Internal mobility

Open roles are advertised internally first, and we back moves between functions where the fit is right.

How we hire

Four steps, and we tell you where you stand

Most processes close within three to four weeks of the advert coming down.

1
Application

Send your CV and a short note on why the role fits. You get a confirmation, not silence.

2
Conversation

A call with HR, then an interview with the hiring manager and the team you would work with.

3
Practical exercise

For technical and commercial roles, a task drawn from the actual job. Nothing artificial.

4
Offer and onboarding

References, a written offer with the terms spelled out, and a structured first few weeks.
